# Chronic Benefits

_Last reviewed: 2026-06. Source: scheme-neutral public guidance._

If you are diagnosed with an ongoing condition, you may be able to register for
**chronic medicine benefits** so your medication is funded from a chronic
benefit rather than your day-to-day savings.

The general process:
1. Your doctor confirms the diagnosis and the ICD-10 code.
2. You (or your doctor) submit a **chronic application** to the scheme.
3. The scheme approves (or declines) chronic cover, often for a set period.
4. The medication may need to be on the scheme **formulary** (approved list) and
   collected from a **DSP pharmacy** to avoid co-payments.

What to confirm:
- "Could my condition qualify for chronic / PMB cover?" (doctor + scheme)
- "Is my medication on the formulary?"
- "Do I need to use a specific DSP pharmacy?"
- "What follow-up tests and consultations are covered?"

Approval must come from the scheme — this tool cannot approve cover.
