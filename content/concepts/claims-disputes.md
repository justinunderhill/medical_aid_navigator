# Claim Rejections & Disputes

_Last reviewed: 2026-06. Source: scheme-neutral public guidance._

A rejected claim is not always the end of the road. Many rejections come down
to missing information, wrong codes, or a step that was skipped.

First steps:
- Get the **written reason** for the rejection from your scheme.
- Check the **ICD-10 and procedure codes** with your provider — errors here are common.
- Check whether **authorisation** was needed and obtained.
- Check whether a **network / DSP** rule applied.

If you think the rejection is wrong:
- Ask the provider to correct and **resubmit** if it was a coding or admin error.
- Follow your scheme's **internal dispute process** first — request it in writing.
- Only after the internal process should external escalation be considered.

What to avoid:
- Making accusations without the written reasons in hand.
- Assuming the outcome — claim outcomes cannot be guaranteed.

This tool helps you organise the right questions and documents. It does not
predict or guarantee the outcome.
