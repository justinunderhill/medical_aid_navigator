# Scenario: Planned Procedure / Scan / Admission

_Last reviewed: 2026-06. Source: scheme-neutral public guidance._

Key navigation points:
- Ask whether pre-authorisation is required (usually yes for scans, admissions, many procedures).
- Gather: diagnosis + ICD-10 code, procedure/tariff codes, provider quote.
- Ask whether the facility is in-network and whether co-payments apply.
- Ask whether the provider charges the medical aid rate or a private rate.
- Keep the authorisation number.

Do not proceed with planned care before checking authorisation — unless it is an emergency.
