# Scenario: Specialist Referral

_Last reviewed: 2026-06. Source: scheme-neutral public guidance._

Key navigation points:
- A GP referral letter is often useful and sometimes required — keep it.
- Confirm whether the specialist is in-network to avoid co-payments.
- Some specialist visits and the procedures they lead to may need authorisation.
- Ask whether the specialist bills at the medical aid rate or a private rate.
- Get the ICD-10 code; if a procedure is planned, get procedure/tariff codes and a quote.

Confirm with the scheme: is cover confirmed, is authorisation needed, are there co-payments.
