# Scenario: Chronic Condition Registration

_Last reviewed: 2026-06. Source: scheme-neutral public guidance._

Key navigation points:
- Diagnosis + ICD-10 code from the doctor is the starting point.
- Submit a chronic application to the scheme (doctor may do this).
- Approval comes from the scheme, often for a set period.
- Confirm the medication is on the formulary and which DSP pharmacy to use.
- Confirm which follow-up consultations and tests are covered.

Some chronic conditions fall under PMB chronic cover (CDL) — confirm with doctor and scheme.
