# Scenario: GP / Pharmacy Clinic Visit

_Last reviewed: 2026-06. Source: scheme-neutral public guidance._

Key navigation points:
- Usually paid from day-to-day or savings benefits — confirm which.
- Network GPs may be cheaper or fully covered; out-of-network may cost more.
- Pharmacy clinics can be a lower-cost option for minor issues.
- Keep the ICD-10 code and any referral if onward care is needed.
