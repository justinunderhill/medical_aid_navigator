# Disclaimer

_Last reviewed: 2026-06. Source: scheme-neutral public guidance._

Medical Aid Navigator is an educational tool that helps South African medical
aid members understand how to navigate their benefits.

It does NOT provide:
- Medical diagnosis or treatment advice
- Legal advice
- Broker or financial advice
- Any guarantee of a claim outcome

It is NOT affiliated with any medical scheme unless explicitly stated.

Always confirm benefits, authorisation, and cover directly with your scheme,
provider, or an accredited broker. If you have urgent symptoms, seek medical
care immediately.

Information about medical aid rules is general, may be out of date, and varies
by scheme and plan. Content is reviewed periodically; benefit rules typically
change each year.
