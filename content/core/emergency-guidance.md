# Emergency Guidance (canonical)

_Last reviewed: 2026-06. Source: scheme-neutral public guidance._

If someone is seriously unwell or injured:
- Get medical care FIRST. Do not wait to check medical aid benefits.
- Call 10177 (ambulance) or 112 (mobile), or go to the nearest emergency room / casualty.
- Emergency stabilisation is handled differently from ordinary planned care; confirm the benefit process with your scheme afterwards.
- Sort out benefits, authorisation, and codes AFTERWARDS.

After the person is stable:
- Contact your scheme to report the emergency visit or admission.
- Keep every document: hospital records, ICD-10 codes, authorisation references, clinical notes.
- Ask your doctor and scheme whether it may fall into a PMB emergency pathway.

This tool does not diagnose emergencies and is not a substitute for emergency
services or a medical professional.
