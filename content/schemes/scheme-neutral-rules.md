# Scheme-Neutral Rules

_Last reviewed: 2026-06. Source: scheme-neutral public guidance._

This MVP is deliberately scheme-neutral. We do not encode plan-specific benefit
limits, formularies, or rules for any named scheme, because those:
- Change every year
- Vary by plan within a scheme
- Cannot be reliably guaranteed from public information

Instead, we teach members the **right questions to ask their own scheme**, and
the **documents and codes to gather**, so they can confirm the specifics
directly.

If a user enters a scheme name, we do NOT invent rules for it. We may
acknowledge the scheme name and still direct the user to confirm with that
scheme.
